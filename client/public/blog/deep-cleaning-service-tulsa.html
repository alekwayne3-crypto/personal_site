What's Actually Included in a Deep Cleaning Service in Tulsa

By Clean Bee Cleaning Co.  ·  Tulsa, Oklahoma

The term "deep cleaning" gets used a lot, but it means different things to different cleaning companies. Some use it to mean they'll spend an extra hour. Others treat it as a genuinely different service category with a much longer task list. If you're booking a deep cleaning service in Tulsa, it's worth knowing upfront exactly what you're getting.

Here's how Clean Bee approaches a deep clean — and how it differs from a standard recurring visit.

Standard Clean vs. Deep Clean

A standard maintenance cleaning is built for homes that are already reasonably clean. The team comes in, wipes surfaces, cleans bathrooms, mops floors, and keeps things tidy. It works great when you're booking every week or two.

A deep clean is designed for homes that need a reset. That might mean you haven't had a professional cleaning in months, you just moved in, you're preparing to sell, or you've had a busy stretch and things have gotten behind. A deep clean takes significantly longer and covers areas that rarely — or never — get touched during a routine visit.

What's Included in a Deep Clean at Clean Bee

Kitchen

  • Inside and outside of the microwave
  • Inside the oven (upon request)
  • Inside the refrigerator (upon request)
  • Cabinet fronts and handles
  • Backsplash scrubbed
  • Range hood and top of refrigerator
  • Sinks scrubbed and faucets polished

Bathrooms

  • Toilet scrubbed inside and out, including base and behind
  • Shower and tub walls scrubbed, grout lines addressed

  • Mirrors and glass cleaned streak-free
  • Vanity drawers and cabinet fronts wiped down
  • Floors mopped, including behind the toilet
  • Exhaust vents dusted

Bedrooms and Living Areas

  • Ceiling fans and light fixtures dusted
  • Baseboards and window sills wiped down
  • Blinds dusted
  • Under furniture vacuumed and mopped (where accessible)
  • Window tracks cleaned
  • Door frames and switch plates wiped
  • Interior windows cleaned

When Should You Book a Deep Clean?

A deep cleaning is a good idea in a few situations:

  • Before starting a recurring service. If you're signing up for regular cleanings, a deep clean as your first appointment sets a strong baseline. After that, maintenance cleanings are faster and more effective.
  • Seasonally. Many Tulsa homeowners book a deep clean once or twice a year — often in spring and again before the holidays. Oklahoma summers mean dusty windows and more tracked-in dirt, so a mid-year reset makes sense.
  • After a long absence or renovation. Construction dust settles into every crevice. Post-reno cleaning is genuinely different work, and a deep clean is the right starting point.
  • When you're moving in or out. See our move-in/move-out cleaning post for specifics on that scenario.

How Long Does a Deep Clean Take?

It depends on the size and condition of the home, but expect a deep clean to take roughly two to four times as long as a standard visit. A 2-bedroom home in good shape might take around three hours. A larger or heavily used home can take six hours or more. We don't rush — the point is to actually get everything.

A Word on Expectations

A deep clean can transform how a home feels and smells. But it's also important to be realistic — a deep clean is not a restoration. Grout that's been neglected for years won't look new, and some stains are permanent. What a deep clean will do is get your home to the cleanest it can reasonably be, and give you a fresh starting point going forward.
