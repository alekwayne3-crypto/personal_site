Standard Cleaning vs. Deep Cleaning in Bixby

If you're setting up a recurring cleaning schedule for the first time, we almost always recommend starting with a deep clean. A standard maintenance clean is meant to keep a clean home clean. A deep clean is what gets you there first. It covers the areas that don't typically get touched week to week: inside the oven and fridge, behind appliances, baseboards, ceiling fans, light fixtures, window sills, and grout lines in the bathrooms.

Bixby homes that haven't had a professional cleaning in six months or more should almost always start with a deep clean before moving to a regular schedule.
