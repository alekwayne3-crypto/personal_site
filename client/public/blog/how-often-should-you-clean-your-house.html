How Often Should You Clean Your House? A Practical, Honest Guide

By Clean Bee Cleaning Co.  ·  Tulsa, Oklahoma

How often should you clean your house? It's a question that sounds like it should have a simple answer, but the truth is it depends on how you live. A couple with no kids and no pets in a small Tulsa apartment has very different cleaning needs than a family of five with two dogs in a four-bedroom home in Broken Arrow. What we can do is give you a realistic breakdown of how often different cleaning tasks should happen, and help you figure out what schedule actually makes sense for your life.

The Short Answer

For most households, a full cleaning of the major areas (bathrooms, kitchen, floors throughout, and surfaces) should happen every one to two weeks. High-use areas, especially the kitchen and main bathrooms, benefit from a quick wipe-down more frequently. And certain tasks, like cleaning inside the oven, tackling baseboards, or washing windows, only need to happen a few times a year. The goal is not to live by a rigid schedule but to understand which areas deteriorate quickly if left alone and which ones can wait without causing real problems.

Daily and Every-Few-Days Tasks

Some things shouldn't wait for the weekly cleaning. Kitchen counters and the stovetop accumulate grease and crumbs quickly, and a brief wipe-down after cooking takes about two minutes and prevents significant buildup. Wiping down the bathroom sink and counter every two to three days keeps it from getting to the point where it requires scrubbing. Floors in high-traffic areas benefit from a quick sweep or vacuum pass a couple of times a week, especially if you have pets. These quick habits are what separate homes that are easy to maintain from ones that require a major effort to get back into shape.

Weekly Cleaning Tasks

The bulk of a home's cleaning is most effectively done on a weekly basis. Toilets should be cleaned every week, and many people find it useful to wipe down the shower and tub walls after use to prevent soap scum buildup. Floors throughout the home should be vacuumed and mopped weekly. Surfaces, shelves, and furniture should be dusted. Kitchen surfaces, including the backsplash and the outside of appliances, should get a thorough wipe-down. If you're handling all of this yourself, plan on at least two to three hours for a medium-sized home. For busy households, this is often where professional cleaning helps most, since it's the work that's most time-consuming and most consistently necessary.

Every 1 to 2 Weeks

  • Full bathroom cleaning (toilets, showers, tubs, sinks)
  • Kitchen counters, stovetop, and exterior of appliances
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped throughout
  • Surfaces and shelves dusted
  • Trash removed from all rooms

Monthly Tasks

Some cleaning tasks don't need weekly attention but should happen once a month to prevent noticeable buildup. Ceiling fans collect dust quickly and can actually spread it around the room when they run if they're not cleaned regularly. Inside the microwave benefits from a thorough cleaning once a month. Cabinet fronts in the kitchen tend to accumulate grease and fingerprints. Baseboards, while they don't get dirty quickly, do build up dust and benefit from a monthly wipe-down. Window sills and tracks also fall into this category.

Monthly

  • Ceiling fans dusted
  • Inside the microwave cleaned
  • Kitchen cabinet fronts wiped down
  • Baseboards wiped throughout
  • Window sills and tracks cleaned
  • Inside the refrigerator checked and wiped down

Seasonal or Twice-a-Year Tasks

These are the tasks most people put off indefinitely, and they're the ones that make up the bulk of a proper deep clean. Inside the oven should be cleaned a few times per year, depending on how much you cook. Interior windows benefit from cleaning twice a year, particularly in Oklahoma where seasonal allergens and dust are a real factor. Behind and under large appliances like the refrigerator and stove collects significant debris and should be pulled out and cleaned at least once a year. The same goes for areas under beds and inside closets. These are also the tasks that get addressed during a professional deep cleaning service.

How Does Your Household Affect the Answer?

The biggest variables are pets, children, and the number of people in the home. Pets shed, track in dirt, and leave dander on every surface. Homes with dogs or cats typically need floors vacuumed at least twice a week. Children create high-traffic zones in bathrooms, eat in more areas of the house, and generally put a lot more wear on surfaces than adults. A household with young kids in Tulsa or Owasso is going to need more frequent cleaning across the board than a household with two adults who spend most of their time at work.
