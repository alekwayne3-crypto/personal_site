Move Out Cleaning Checklist for Tulsa Rentals — What Landlords Actually Look For

By Clean Bee Cleaning Co.  ·  Tulsa, Oklahoma

Moving is stressful enough without worrying about losing your security deposit over cleaning. Whether you're leaving a house in Jenks, an apartment in midtown Tulsa, or a rental in Broken Arrow, most landlords and property managers have a specific move out cleaning checklist they walk through during the final inspection. Knowing what's on that list — and making sure every item is actually done — is the difference between getting your deposit back and getting a bill in the mail.

Why a Thorough Move Out Clean Matters in Tulsa

Oklahoma landlord-tenant law allows landlords to deduct the cost of cleaning from your security deposit if the unit is not returned in the same general condition it was received, accounting for normal wear and tear. "Normal wear and tear" does not cover a dirty oven, grimy bathroom tile, or carpet that smells like pets. Landlords in Tulsa, Owasso, and Sapulpa increasingly hire professional cleaning crews after a tenant leaves and charge the cost back to the tenant if the unit doesn't meet their standards.

A professional move out cleaning is almost always cheaper than the cleaning fee your landlord will charge, and it gives you documentation that the job was done. Here is a thorough move out cleaning checklist organized by room, along with notes on what inspectors tend to notice first.

Kitchen

The kitchen is the room most likely to cost you money if it's not cleaned properly. Grease buildup on surfaces, a dirty oven interior, and a refrigerator with odors are the three things property managers in Tulsa flag most often.

Kitchen Checklist

  • Oven cleaned inside, including racks and the area under the heating element
  • Stovetop and burners degreased thoroughly
  • Refrigerator emptied, wiped inside and out, and all shelves and drawers cleaned
  • Microwave cleaned inside and outside
  • Cabinet interiors and exteriors wiped down
  • Backsplash scrubbed free of grease and food splatter
  • Sink and faucet scrubbed and polished
  • Countertops cleaned, including corners and the area behind the faucet
  • Floor mopped, including under the stove and refrigerator if accessible
  • Exhaust hood or microwave vent filter cleaned or noted if not cleanable

Bathrooms

Bathrooms are inspected closely. Soap scum on shower walls, hard water stains around the toilet base, and a mirror with streaks are common issues that lead to deductions. If the grout in the shower is dark with mildew, cleaning it properly takes time and the right products.

Bathroom Checklist

  • Toilet scrubbed inside and out, including under the rim, the base, and behind the tank
  • Shower and tub walls scrubbed, with special attention to grout lines
  • Shower door or curtain rod cleaned; glass doors polished streak-free
  • Sink and vanity top cleaned, faucet polished
  • Inside and outside of vanity cabinet wiped down
  • Mirror cleaned streak-free
  • Exhaust fan vent cover dusted or wiped
  • Floor mopped, including the area behind the toilet
  • Caulk areas cleaned (can look discolored even if clean — note this vs. mold)

Bedrooms and Living Areas

These rooms often look easier than they are because clutter hides how much surface area actually needs to be cleaned. Once furniture is removed, baseboards, window sills, and wall scuffs become very visible.

Bedroom and Living Area Checklist

  • Baseboards wiped down throughout all rooms
  • Window sills and tracks cleaned
  • Interior windows wiped down
  • Blinds dusted or wiped
  • Ceiling fans dusted
  • Light switch plates wiped
  • Closet shelves and rods wiped down
  • Carpet vacuumed; any stains addressed (professional carpet cleaning may be required)
  • Hard floors swept, vacuumed, and mopped
  • Walls spot-checked for marks; most can be addressed with a damp cloth

Often-Missed Areas That Show Up in Inspections

A few spots consistently get missed during move-out cleans, even by people who think they've been thorough. The area under the refrigerator and stove collects years of debris. Garage floors and utility spaces are often not cleaned at all. The inside of the dishwasher is sometimes forgotten. Air vent covers in bedrooms accumulate dust. And the front door, exterior light fixtures near the entrance, and any outdoor spaces that were part of the rental should be included in your move out cleaning checklist as well.
