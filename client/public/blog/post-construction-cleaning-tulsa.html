Post Construction Cleaning in Tulsa — Getting Your Property Move-In Ready

By Clean Bee Cleaning Co.  ·  Tulsa, Oklahoma

Post construction cleaning in Tulsa is a completely different kind of work than standard residential cleaning. After a new build or a significant renovation, the property looks like it should be almost ready — the walls are painted, the floors are in, the fixtures are installed. But there's a layer of construction dust, debris, adhesive residue, paint overspray, and fine particulate covering nearly every surface in the home. Getting it truly clean requires a specific process, the right equipment, and enough time to do it properly.

Why Construction Dust Is a Special Problem

Construction dust is extremely fine and settles on everything: inside cabinets, along window tracks, on top of light fixtures, in HVAC vents, and deep in the fibers of any carpet. It doesn't behave like regular household dust. You can't just wipe a surface once and move on because the dust resettles from the air as you work. A proper post construction cleaning process accounts for this by working top-down and in stages, allowing the dust to settle before doing the final pass on floors and lower surfaces.

This is why post construction cleaning in Tulsa typically takes much longer than a standard clean of the same size property, and why it's not something that responds well to being rushed.

What's Included in a Post Construction Clean

Every post-construction job is a little different depending on the scope of the work done, but there are core areas that always need to be addressed. All surfaces in every room get wiped down, including walls, window frames, door frames, baseboards, and trim. Cabinets and drawers are cleaned inside and out. Windows are cleaned of construction film, tape residue, and smudges. Fixtures and hardware are wiped down and polished. Floors are cleaned last, after all other surfaces have been addressed, to capture everything that fell during the cleaning process.

Bathrooms in a newly constructed or renovated space often have grout haze on tile floors and walls, which requires specific products and technique to remove properly. Kitchens may have adhesive residue from protective film on appliances, tile grout haze, and paint marks near trim lines. These are things a general cleaning crew might miss, but they're standard in a dedicated post-construction process.

New Construction vs. Renovation — Is the Process Different?

Somewhat. A new construction clean typically covers the entire property from scratch, since nothing was previously occupied or cleaned. A renovation clean focuses on the affected areas and may need to work around furniture and belongings that weren't moved during the project. Both require the same attention to detail, but the scope and approach are slightly different. How to Time Your Post Construction Clean

The best time to schedule a post construction cleaning in Tulsa is after all trade work is completely finished and before anyone moves in. That means no more contractors returning to touch up, no furniture being moved in, and no people living in the space. Once we've completed the clean, the property is ready for occupancy or photography. If you schedule us before all the trades are done, you'll likely need a second clean after the final work is completed.

For new construction homes in growing areas like Catoosa, Claremore, and south Broken Arrow, timing the cleaning to coordinate with the closing date is something we handle regularly. We know how to work with tight timelines when it comes to builder and developer schedules.
